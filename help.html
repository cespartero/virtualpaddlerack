What it does

Court Manager replaces the paddle rack on club nights with a board on the club's computer or tablet at the desk. This guide covers the offline version (v1.0): a single file, Court-Manager-v1.0.html, that runs without internet. Players and admins all use that one device. Data is saved in that device's browser, and other phones don't see the board.

The main way to use it is Virtual Paddle Rack mode. It runs all night, and people can join or leave at any time.

Round robin and court up down tournaments are marked Coming soon and can't be started yet.

For players

  1. Open the board on the club device at the desk.
  2. Join the queue. Type your name in the Your name box on the right and tap Join. You go to the end of the queue as New. If someone with the same name is already there, add your surname initial, for example Sam P.
  3. Watch your place. The queue shows your position number and your status. The line above it, Next to be allocated, names the four players going on next.
  4. Get called to a court. When a court is allocated, your name appears on that court. You have 1 minute of walk-on time (a green Walk on time countdown) before the match clock starts.
  5. Play. The court shows Match in progress and the match time.
  6. Rejoin automatically. When the result is entered, you go back to the end of the queue as a Winner or a Grinner. You don't need to type your name again.

Taking a break? Tap Sit out next to your name. You keep your place but are skipped until you tap Back in.

How a court is filled

A free court takes new players first, then up to 2 Winners and 2 Grinners, longest waiting first.

StatusWhat it means
NewJust joined and hasn't played yet. Goes on first.
WinnerWon their last game.
GrinnerLost their last game.

For a doubles court, the board picks four players in this order

  1. Anyone marked New, in queue order.
  2. Up to 2 Winners and up to 2 Grinners, longest waiting first.
  3. If those limits leave a spot empty, whoever has waited longest fills it, so a court never sits idle.

The four are split into two teams so that two winners aren't paired together. Players who are sitting out are skipped and keep their place. A singles court works the same way with 2 players and a limit of 1 Winner and 1 Grinner.

Finishing a match

Tap End match on the court when the game is over. The match clock stops, then:

  • Winners only session: tap the team that won.
  • Capture scores session: enter both teams' scores and tap Save result. The higher score wins. Level scores can't be saved, so play a deciding point.

All four players go back to the end of the queue, winners ahead of losers. The court shows as free and ready for the next four.

Entered the wrong result? Tap Undo in the yellow bar above the courts. Tap Cancel in the End match panel to go back to the game without saving anything.

For admins

Unlock admin. Tap Admin Menu (top right) and enter the admin PIN. The tag beside it changes from Locked to Unlocked. The unlock lasts 30 minutes on that device. Tap Lock admin in the menu when you step away.

Before the session

  1. Players can join the queue before the session starts.
  2. To bring back last session's group in one go, tap Auto add all previous session players under the Join box. It needs admin unlock, adds everyone from the last session as New, and skips anyone already in the queue.

Start the session

  1. In the Admin Menu, tap Start new session.
  2. Choose Virtual Paddle Rack mode.
  3. Choose Match scores: Capture scores to record scores, or Winners only to just tap the winning team. The board remembers your last choice.
  4. Set Courts available and tap Start session. Courts start empty.

Allocate courts

Button on a free courtWhat it does
Allocate DoublesPuts the next 4 players on, using the rules above.
Manual doubles allocationLets you pick the 4 players and teams yourself. It starts with the board's choice.
Allocate SinglesPuts the next 2 players on.
Manual SinglesLets you pick the 2 players.

Fix mistakes on a court

  • Unallocate, during the 1-minute walk-on only, sends the players back to their old places in the queue.
  • Cancel match, after walk-on, records the match as cancelled with no winner. Nobody's stats change, and the players go to the back of the queue.

Manage the queue (admin unlocked). Drag players by the ≡ handle to reorder them, change a player's status from the dropdown, or tap Remove.

End the session. In the Admin Menu, tap End session. This clears the courts and queue and saves the night as Last session results on the Board tab. Games still on court aren't counted.

The tabs

The tabs under the header switch between views. Anyone can use them, and no PIN is needed.

TabWhat it shows
BoardCourts and the queue. When no session is running, it also shows Last session results.
StandingsEvery player's current status (Playing on Ct X or In queue · Winner), games played, won and lost, win %, and their last 5 results. Scored sessions add points, +/− and Avg PPG (average points per game).
Session statusHow busy each court has been during the session.
Match historyEvery result, newest first, with court, teams, score (when captured) and match length.

While a session runs, its mode is shown in bold under the Court Manager title.

Tips and troubleshooting

  • "Already on the board" when joining: someone has that name. Add a surname initial.
  • A court says "Waiting for 4 players": fewer than 4 people are waiting who aren't sitting out. Allocate Singles if 2 or 3 are ready.
  • Someone left without playing: an admin taps Remove next to their name, or the player taps Sit out if they're coming back.
  • Admin buttons missing: admin has locked again after 30 minutes. Tap Admin Menu and re-enter the PIN.
  • Board not updating: refresh the page. Data is saved in the browser on that device, so nothing is lost. A second window on the same computer (for example a big screen) stays in sync; other devices don't.
  • Change the PIN: Admin Menu → Change PIN. The new PIN is saved on that device.

Back up your data. Admin Menu → Download backup saves the queue, results and settings to a file. Use Restore backup to load it on another computer, or after the browser's data has been cleared. Don't clear the browser's site data or switch browsers without a backup: the board would start empty.
